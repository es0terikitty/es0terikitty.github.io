year at the time, while their elder brother Dmitri was twenty-seven. First of all, I must explain that this young man, Alyosha, was
not a fanatic, and, in my opinion at least, was not even a mystic.
I may as well give my full opinion from the beginning. He
was simply an early lover of humanity, and that he adopted the
monastic life was simply because at that time it struck him, so to
