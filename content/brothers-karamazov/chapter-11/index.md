“You apply them to us, and look upon us as socialists?” Father
Païssy asked directly, without beating about the bush.
But before Pyotr Alexandrovitch could think what to answer,
the door opened, and the guest so long expected, Dmitri Fyodorovitch, came in. They had, in fact, given up expecting him, and
his sudden appearance caused some surprise for a moment.
