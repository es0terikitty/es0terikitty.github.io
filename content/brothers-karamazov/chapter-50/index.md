She was sitting in the kitchen with her grandmother; they were
both just going to bed. Relying on Nazar Ivanovitch, they had
not locked themselves in. Mitya ran in, pounced on Fenya and
seized her by the throat.
“Speak at once! Where is she? With whom is she now, at
Mokroe?” he roared furiously.
Both the women squealed.
“Aie! I'll tell you. Aie! Dmitri Fyodorovitch, darling, I'll tell
you everything directly, I won't hide anything,” gabbled Fenya,
frightened to death; “she's gone to Mokroe, to her officer.”
“What officer?” roared Mitya.
“To her officer, the same one she used to know, the one who
threw her over five years ago,” cackled Fenya, as fast as she
could speak.
