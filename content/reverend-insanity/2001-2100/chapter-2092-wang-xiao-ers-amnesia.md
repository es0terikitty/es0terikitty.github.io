<p>In an ordinary and common cave, the four great obscenities of Central Continent chose to rest in this place. Wang Xiao Er was an ordinary shepherd, but life was extremely cruel to him. During the battle to pursue Fang Yuan, the aftermath caused the mountain he lived on to be destroyed, all of his family members were dead. But for some reason, Wang Xiao Er did not die, he even fell into this peculiar state. &quot;Think about it, when we first met him, the grass and flowers around him were blooming and dying constantly. Plop, plop... the four Gu Masters knelt down on the ground, shouting: &quot;Immortal, spare us!&quot; Wang Xiao Er was shocked: &quot;Who are you guys? Where am I?
