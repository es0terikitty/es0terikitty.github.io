<p>Continuous explosions resounded like thunder. Every explosion shook the whole of Sacred Feather City. Rank six slavery Immortal Gu could only enslave a rank six Gu Immortal. &quot;It is also because Zhou Zhong is a featherman and not a human. His intelligence is inferior just by a bit, if I were to enslave a human Gu Immortal, I might have failed.&quot; &quot;I had cultivated soul path previously and have some foundation in regards to my soul. <p>Shortly afterwards, Zhou Zhong&#x27;s voice arrived again: &quot;Yu Fei is dead, turned into ashes.&quot; At present, Zhou Zhong was the only one controlling Sacred Feather City alone, as such, this was accomplished easily. &quot;Good, do all you can to control the Immortal Gu House and get away from the battle!&quot; Fang Yuan sent another command.
