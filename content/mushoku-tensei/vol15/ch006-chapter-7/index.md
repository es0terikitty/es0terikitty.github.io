human, which you basically just climbed into. Once you were feeding
it mana, it acted as an extension of your own body, so you could
manually close the rear armor over you. That section of armor also
contained a special magic circle that would automatically eject you
out of the back if you spoke the correct command.
We’d mounted the ‘Gatling gun’ on the right arm. The modified
Magic Implement now automatically fired off Stone Cannon spells
whenever I wanted it to. When I fed it as much mana as I could, it
was capable of shooting my very deadliest Stone Cannon ten times
per second. It would reduce your average monster to a bloody mist
in the blink of an eye.
This was my main plan for coping with Orsted’s Disturb Magic.
On the left hand, we’d mounted a Stone of Absorption. For the
most part, I was planning to ward off Orsted’s spells with Disturb
Magic, but it was possible I wouldn’t have the time if things got too
