The board was absolutely covered with dozens of pieces of
paper. There was a mountain of work that needed doing apparently.

F or E, and there weren’t any particularly epic-looking quests in those
categories. The majority were just odd jobs around the city—stuff
like organizing a warehouse, helping out in someone’s kitchen, basic
bookkeeping, looking for a lost pet, and exterminating insects.
None of them looked especially challenging, but the rewards
were also low.
